## 4. Moderator principle

Prefer behavior over opinion.

Do:
> “Show me how you would find out what happened.”

Avoid:
> “Do you like this dashboard?”

Do not explain the interface during the task unless the session is explicitly testing assisted onboarding.

## 7. Follow-up questions

Use after behavior has been observed.

- What did you expect to happen there?
- What information were you looking for?
- What would you normally do next in your real workflow?
- Was anything important missing?
- What, if anything, made you uncertain?
- How would you describe this feature/system to a colleague?

Avoid leading questions that reveal intended behavior.

---

## 8. Capture model
