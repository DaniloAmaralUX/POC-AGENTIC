# Paperclip Company Goal Template

Use this as the starting goal for a POC company. Replace placeholders with project-specific language supported by evidence.

```text
Transform the available source material for <PROJECT> into a traceable, high-quality design + front-end POC that demonstrates the smallest credible vertical slice of the product opportunity.

Follow the canonical process defined in:
https://github.com/DaniloAmaralUX/POC-AGENTIC

Required outcome:
1. source/evidence inventory;
2. claims separated from inference and assumptions;
3. traceable requirements baseline;
4. POC Brief and POC PRD;
5. domain model and critical journeys;
6. information architecture and task flows;
7. high-fidelity design specification;
8. executable front-end POC;
9. verification report with explicit real-vs-mocked behavior;
10. final decision record.

Constraints:
- Do not claim direct user validation unless representative users were actually involved.
- Do not invent unsupported business or technical requirements.
- Do not silently promote assumptions into facts.
- Do not add generic SaaS features without evidence or an explicit Design Hypothesis.
- Preserve source terminology where it matters.
- Keep all major decisions traceable upstream.
- The front-end may use deterministic fixtures/mocks when integrations are unavailable, but must never present mocked capability as real.

Definition of success:
The final POC is understandable, credible and executable; another agent can trace every important product decision back to evidence, derivation or an explicitly labeled hypothesis; and the delivery includes both design and front-end without implementation drift.
```

## Goal-writing rule

A company goal should describe the outcome and quality bar, not prescribe a full feature list before requirements work is complete.
